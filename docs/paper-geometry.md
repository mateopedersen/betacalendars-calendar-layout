# Paper geometry

The standard formats are A4 210×297 mm, A5 148×210 mm, US Letter 215.9×279.4 mm, and US Legal 215.9×355.6 mm. Landscape swaps the dimensions. The default page margins are 10 mm on each edge; the title and weekday-header bands reserve 16 mm and 8 mm. Per-cell writing area subtracts an inner padding allowance on each side of the cell. These are explicit model parameters, not printer-specific safe-area guarantees.
