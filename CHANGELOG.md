# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-07

- Initial C++17 header-only release.
- Gregorian civil-date weekday and month topology for years 1–9999.
- Seven week starts, natural and fixed grids, adjacent-date policies.
- Undated grid and printable geometry metrics for common paper formats.
- CMake package export, optional CLI, and exhaustive calendar tests.
